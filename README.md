# Community Support

Welcome to the Community Support repository! This space is dedicated to fostering collaboration, discussions, and support for community-driven projects.

## Overview

This repository serves as a central hub for members to connect, share ideas, and contribute to ongoing community initiatives. Whether you're looking to join the organization, participate in discussions, or stay updated on events, you're in the right place.

## Features

- Open discussions on community projects
- Weekly standup meetings
- Active presence on social media platforms

## Getting Involved

**Contribute:**  
Feel free to participate in discussions, suggest new ideas, or help with ongoing projects. 

Create a new issue in the [Issues](https://github.com/reactdeveloperske/community-support/issues) tab expressing your interest in joining the organization. Our team will review your request and get back to you promptly.

We welcome contributions from everyone!

## Weekly Standups

We hold weekly standup meetings every **Wednesdays, Thursdays and Sundays at 8:00 PM**.  
Stay informed about meeting details and updates through our community channels:

- [Twitter](https://twitter.com/reactdevske)
- [Telegram](https://bit.ly/joinreactdevske)

Everyone is welcome to join!

## Contact

For any questions or further information, please reach out via our [Twitter](https://twitter.com/reactdevske) or [Telegram](https://bit.ly/joinreactdevske) accounts.
